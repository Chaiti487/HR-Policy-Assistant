# Leave Policy

## Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leave days per calendar year.

A maximum of 5 unused casual leave days can be carried forward to the following year.

Casual leave cannot be converted into cash.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 sick leave days per year.

Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Vacation Leave

Employees receive 20 vacation leave days per year.

A maximum of 10 unused vacation leave days may be carried forward.
